# NovaDesk Electronics: Customer Help Center

NovaDesk Electronics is a fictional online store that sells laptops, monitors, keyboards and accessories. This document is the official customer FAQ.

## About Us
NovaDesk was founded in 2019 and is based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. We ship to Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and Thailand. Our mission is to make reliable work-from-home gear affordable.

## Opening Hours and Contact
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 (GMT+8). On Saturdays, support is open from 10:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.
You can reach us by email at support@novadesk.example or by live chat on our website. We reply to emails within 24 business hours.

## Shipping
Standard shipping within Malaysia takes 3 to 5 business days and costs RM 10. Orders above RM 200 ship for free within Malaysia.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs RM 25.
International shipping to Singapore, Indonesia and Thailand takes 5 to 10 business days and costs RM 40.
Orders placed after 15:00 are processed on the next business day.

## Order Tracking
After your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can track your order on the "My Orders" page of your account. If your tracking number has not updated for 5 business days, contact support.

## Returns and Refunds
You can return most items within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging.
Opened software, gift cards and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
To start a return, open "My Orders", choose the item and click "Request Return". We will email you a return label.
Refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive and inspect the item.
Return shipping is free if the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item. Otherwise, a RM 15 return fee is deducted from the refund.

## Warranty
All laptops and monitors come with a 2-year NovaDesk warranty. Keyboards, mice and other accessories have a 1-year warranty.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage or damage from unofficial repairs.
To make a warranty claim, email support with your order number, a photo or video of the problem, and a short description.

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, online banking (FPX) and cash on delivery within Malaysia. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to RM 1,000.

## Account and Password
To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the link we email you. The link expires after 30 minutes.
You can delete your account at any time from "Account Settings". Deleting your account does not cancel open orders.

## Privacy
We only use your personal data to process orders and provide support. We never sell your data to third parties. You can request a copy of your data by emailing privacy@novadesk.example.
